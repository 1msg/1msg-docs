There are two ways to connect a WABA:

## WABA from 1MSG

Message fees are paid through 1MSG at [WhatsApp rates + a 5% markup](../../platform/billing/messaging-costs.md#pricing)

* Free test channel
* Message history
* Media storage
* Delivery log
* Developer tools and SDK
* Support

[Connect WABA from 1MSG](./waba-from-1msg.md)

## Bring your own WABA

1MSG does not charge for messages — you pay Meta directly.

* 30-day free trial for a production channel
* Message history
* Media storage
* Delivery log
* Developer tools and SDK
* Support

[Connect Bring your own WABA](./bring-your-own-waba.md)
